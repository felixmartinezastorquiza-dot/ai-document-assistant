BRIGHTSMILE DENTAL - PATIENT POLICIES
BrightSmile Dental is a fictional sample company created for a portfolio demo.

1. APPOINTMENT CANCELLATIONS
Please give us at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment.
Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice are charged a late cancellation fee of $50.
Patients who miss two appointments without notice will be asked to prepay future appointments.

2. LATE ARRIVALS
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment so that other patients are not delayed.

3. PAYMENT
Payment is due at the time of service.
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and HSA/FSA cards.
We do not accept personal checks.

4. INSURANCE
We are in-network with most major PPO dental insurance plans.
We do not accept HMO dental plans or Medicaid.
Please send us your insurance details at least 48 hours before your first visit so we can verify your benefits.
Any amount not covered by your insurance is due at the time of service.

5. MINORS
Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at their first visit.

6. DENTAL RECORDS
Copies of your dental records and X-rays are sent within 5 business days of a written request, free of charge.

7. PRIVACY
Your health information is kept confidential and is only shared with your consent or when required by law.
