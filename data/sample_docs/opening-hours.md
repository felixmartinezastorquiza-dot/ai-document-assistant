# BrightSmile Dental — Opening Hours & Contact

*BrightSmile Dental is a fictional sample company created for a portfolio demo.*

## Opening hours

| Day               | Hours               |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Monday – Thursday | 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM   |
| Friday            | 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM   |
| Saturday          | 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM   |
| Sunday            | Closed              |

The clinic is closed on national public holidays. Holiday closures are announced on our website two weeks in advance.

## Lunch break

The front desk is closed for lunch from 12:30 PM to 1:15 PM, Monday to Friday. Phone calls during this time go to voicemail and are returned the same afternoon.

## After-hours dental emergencies

For dental emergencies outside opening hours (severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out tooth), call our after-hours emergency line at (555) 014-2399. A dentist on call will return your call within 30 minutes.
