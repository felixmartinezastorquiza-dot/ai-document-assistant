# BrightSmile Dental — Frequently Asked Questions

*BrightSmile Dental is a fictional sample company created for a portfolio demo.*

### Are you accepting new patients?
Yes. You can book online at brightsmile-dental.example/book or call us. Your first visit includes a full exam and X-rays and takes about 60 minutes.

### Do you treat children?
Yes, we see children from age 3. We recommend a child's first dental visit by their third birthday.

### Can I get a same-day emergency appointment?
Yes. If you call before 11:00 AM on a weekday, we will do our best to see you the same day.

### Is there parking?
Yes. Free patient parking is available behind the building. The clinic is on the second floor and is wheelchair accessible by elevator.

### What languages do you speak?
Our team speaks English and Spanish.

### How often should I get a dental cleaning?
For most patients, we recommend a cleaning and check-up every 6 months.

### Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?
Yes. We offer nitrous oxide and IV sedation. See our treatment preparation guide for details.

### Do I get a reminder before my appointment?
Yes. We send a text message reminder 2 days before your appointment.
