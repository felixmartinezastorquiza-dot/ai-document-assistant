# BrightSmile Dental — Preparing for Your Treatment

*BrightSmile Dental is a fictional sample company created for a portfolio demo.*

## Routine cleaning

- Brush and floss as usual before your appointment.
- Bring a list of any medications you currently take.
- A routine cleaning takes about 45 minutes.

## Tooth extraction

**Before the procedure**

- If you will receive sedation, do not eat or drink anything for 6 hours before your appointment.
- Tell us about any blood-thinning medication at least one week before the extraction.

**After the procedure**

- Do not use straws, smoke, or rinse forcefully for 72 hours. This protects the blood clot and helps prevent a painful condition called dry socket.
- Eat soft, cool foods for the first 2 days.
- Mild swelling is normal for up to 3 days. Call us if bleeding continues after 24 hours.

## Teeth whitening

- We recommend a professional cleaning within 2 weeks before whitening for the best results.
- Avoid coffee, tea, red wine and other staining foods and drinks for 48 hours after whitening.
- Temporary tooth sensitivity is common and usually disappears within 1 to 3 days.
- Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.

## Root canal treatment

- Eat a normal meal before your appointment. Root canals are done under local anesthesia, so you can eat beforehand.
- If we prescribed antibiotics, take the full course as instructed.
- Your mouth may stay numb for 2 to 4 hours afterwards. Avoid chewing until the numbness wears off.

## Sedation options

- **Nitrous oxide ("laughing gas"):** wears off within minutes. You can drive yourself home.
- **IV sedation:** you must arrange for an adult to drive you home. You cannot drive, work or operate machinery for the rest of the day.
